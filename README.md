# Our Golden Memories

A luxury interactive digital friendship scrapbook for three best friends: Peris, Asma and Ruth.

## About the project
Our Golden Memories keeps our special moments in one place: photographs, videos, personal letters, captions and unforgettable memories. It has an elegant black and gold design and opens with a shining star.

## Features
- Shining star opening animation
- Black and gold luxury design
- Photo and video gallery with captions
- Our story, with notes from each friend
- Personal letters from Peris, Asma and Ruth
- Unforgettable moments written by all three of us
- Music player with "Count on Me" by Bruno Mars
- Editable pages: tap any text to write
- Upload your own photos and videos
- Save as PDF and download as HTML

## How to use
1. Open the live link in any browser.
2. Tap "Open our memories".
3. Use the menu to move between Cover, Our story, Gallery, Letters and Moments.
4. Tap any text to edit it, and use "Add a moment" to write your own.
5. Use "Send my writing", "Save as PDF" or "Download HTML" at the top.
